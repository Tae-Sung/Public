## The two talks

The 2023 deck is reference [0] of the 2026 deck. Both use Galileo's three
interlocutors — Simplicio, Sagredo and Salviati — as a device for staging a
disagreement rather than asserting a conclusion.

> **A note on Sagredo.** The 2023 deck glosses the three names as
> *Simple · Sacred · Sage*, chosen for alliteration. The 2026 deck uses
> *The Simple · The Thoughtful · The Sage*, which is closer to the roles the
> characters actually play in Galileo's dialogues: Sagredo is the intelligent
> layman who weighs both sides, not a sacred figure. The 2026 gloss supersedes
> the 2023 one; the earlier deck is left unaltered as a record.

The 2023 deck was prepared without AI assistance, as its disclaimer states.
The 2026 deck describes a system built with one. The three years between them
are the point.
